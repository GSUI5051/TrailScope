Import a Trail Track

GPX, KML, and KMZ files are supported. The page parses coordinates and elevation data, calculates distance, grades, total ascent/descent, and generates a visual report. Images, videos, and audio attachments inside KMZ are ignored, while text waypoints are preserved. All processing is done locally in your browser.

Imported tracks may be hand-planned rather than recorded walks. Evaluate route conditions and personal safety carefully before hiking.

The track does not contain valid elevation data, so elevation-based analysis is unavailable.
